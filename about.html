About Big Mike

Dedicated to helping you become the strongest version of yourself.

My Story

From Passion to Purpose

I'm 19, and I've been obsessed with training for the past 5 years. What I lack in decades of experience, I make up for with genuine passion and a fresh perspective on fitness.

I got into personal training because I saw too many people struggling with the same cookie-cutter programs that don't fit real life. I wanted to help people find what actually works for them.

My approach is simple: meet you where you are, build sustainable habits, and get you stronger without burning you out. I'm still learning and growing as a trainer, and I'm honest about that—but I bring energy, dedication, and a real commitment to your progress.

When I'm not training clients, you'll find me in the gym working on my own goals or studying new training methods.
