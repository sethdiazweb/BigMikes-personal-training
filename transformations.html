Your Transformation Journey

Every transformation starts with a single step. Here's exactly what your journey with Big Mike's looks like from day one to reaching your goals.

1

Free Consultation

We start with a no-pressure conversation about your goals, your current fitness level, and what's held you back before. This isn't a sales pitch - it's about understanding exactly what you want to achieve and whether we're a good fit to work together.

  • Discuss your specific goals (weight loss, muscle gain, strength, etc.)
  • Review your schedule and availability
  • Talk about any injuries or limitations
  • Answer all your questions - no obligation
2

Custom Plan Creation

Based on our consultation, I'll build a program specifically for you - not a cookie-cutter template. Your plan considers your goals, experience level, available equipment, and the time you can realistically commit.

  • Personalized workout programming
  • Realistic timeline and milestones
  • Nutrition guidance that fits your lifestyle
  • Progress tracking system
3

Training & Support

This is where the work happens. Whether we're training together in person or I'm coaching you remotely, you'll never feel lost or unsure what to do. I'm here to guide every rep, answer every question, and keep you accountable.

  • Expert form coaching and technique
  • Regular check-ins and program adjustments
  • Direct access to me for questions
  • Motivation when you need it most
4

Results & Beyond

As you progress, we'll celebrate your wins, adjust your program to keep challenging you, and build habits that stick long after you've hit your goals. This isn't about quick fixes - it's about changing your life.

  • Track measurable progress together
  • Progressive program updates
  • Build sustainable long-term habits
  • Optional: Share your success story
