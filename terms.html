Services Provided

Big Mike's Personal Training provides personal fitness training services, including:

  • One-on-one personal training sessions
  • Group training sessions
  • Online coaching and programming
  • Nutrition guidance and meal planning support
  • Fitness assessments and goal setting

Services are provided in the Elizabethtown, PA area (17022) for in-person training, with online coaching available nationwide.

Booking and Scheduling

Free Consultation

Your first consultation is free with no obligation. This allows us to discuss your goals, assess your needs, and determine if we're a good fit for working together.

Session Scheduling

Sessions are scheduled in advance through direct communication or our online booking system. We will work together to find times that fit your schedule.

Cancellation Policy

24-Hour Notice Required: Please provide at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule a session. Sessions cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice may be charged in full or deducted from your package.

Exceptions may be made for emergencies or illness on a case-by-case basis. Please communicate with us as soon as possible if circumstances arise.

Sessions Never Expire

When you purchase a training package, your sessions never expire. Life happens, and we understand that you may need to pause your training. Your purchased sessions will always be available when you're ready to use them.

Payment Terms

Pricing

Current pricing is available on our Pricing page. Prices are subject to change, but any changes will not affect sessions already purchased.

Payment Methods

We accept various payment methods including cash, check, Venmo, PayPal, and credit cards. Payment details will be discussed during your consultation.

Package Payments

Training packages may be paid in full upfront or through agreed-upon payment plans. Payment terms will be confirmed before training begins.

Refund Policy

First Session Guarantee: If you're not completely satisfied after your first paid training session, we'll refund your money - no questions asked. We want you to feel confident that this is the right fit for you.

For multi-session packages, unused sessions may be refunded on a prorated basis at our discretion. Please discuss any concerns with us directly.

Medical Clearance

Before beginning any exercise program, you should consult with your physician or healthcare provider, especially if you:

  • Have any chronic health conditions
  • Have a history of heart disease or high blood pressure
  • Are pregnant or postpartum
  • Have any injuries or physical limitations
  • Are over 40 and have been inactive

You agree to honestly disclose any health conditions, injuries, or limitations that may affect your ability to exercise safely. You also agree to inform us immediately if you experience any pain, discomfort, or unusual symptoms during training.
