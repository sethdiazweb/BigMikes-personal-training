Training Approach & Methods

What's your training philosophy?

I believe in building sustainable habits that fit your real life. No cookie-cutter programs or unrealistic expectations. We'll focus on progressive strength training, functional movements, and creating a routine you can actually stick with long-term.

My approach is honest and straightforward - I'm here to help you get stronger and feel better, not sell you on quick fixes that don't work.

What training methods do you use?

I focus on compound movements and progressive overload - the fundamentals that actually work. We'll incorporate strength training, conditioning work, and mobility exercises based on your specific goals.

Everything is tailored to your fitness level, available equipment, and what your body responds to best. I stay current with training methods and apply what's proven to work.

Do you work with beginners?

Absolutely! I love working with beginners. We'll start with the basics, build proper form, and progress at a pace that feels challenging but achievable. No judgment, no intimidation - just solid coaching to help you build confidence and strength.

Can you help with specific goals like weight loss or muscle building?

Yes! Whether you want to lose fat, build muscle, get stronger, or improve athletic performance, we'll create a program designed around your specific goals. I'll also provide nutrition guidance to support your training.

Scheduling & Availability

What are your training hours?

I'm available Monday through Friday from 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM, Saturdays from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and Sundays from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. I'm flexible and can work around your schedule to find times that work best for you. Early morning and evening slots are popular, so book ahead to secure your preferred times.

How far in advance do I need to schedule sessions?

I recommend booking at least a week in advance to get your preferred time slots. That said, I'll do my best to accommodate last-minute requests when my schedule allows.

What if I need to reschedule or cancel a session?

Life happens! You can reschedule sessions with 24 hours notice at no charge. Sessions cancelled with less than 24 hours notice will be counted as completed. This policy helps me manage my schedule and serve all my clients fairly.

Do unused sessions roll over to the next month?

Sessions expire at the end of each month, so I encourage you to use them! Consistency is key to seeing results. If you know you'll need to miss sessions due to vacation or other commitments, let me know in advance and we can work something out.

Online vs In-Person Training

Where do you train clients?

I'm based in Elizabethtown, PA and train clients at local gyms and private facilities. I can also come to your home gym if you have the necessary equipment. Online coaching is available for clients anywhere.

How does online training work?

Online coaching includes custom workout programs delivered through an app, weekly video check-ins where we review your form and progress, and ongoing messaging support. You'll record your workouts and send me videos, and I'll provide feedback and adjustments as needed.

It's perfect for people who travel frequently, have unpredictable schedules, or simply prefer training on their own time with expert guidance.

Can I switch between online and in-person training?

Absolutely! Many of my clients do a hybrid approach - in-person sessions a couple times a month with online coaching in between. We can customize a plan that works for your schedule and budget.

First Session & Getting Started

What should I expect in my first session?

Your first session starts with a fitness assessment where we'll discuss your goals, health history, current fitness level, and any injuries or limitations. Then we'll do a movement assessment to see how your body moves and identify areas to work on.

After that, we'll dive into a workout so you can experience my training style. I'll teach you proper form on key exercises and we'll establish a baseline for your strength and conditioning.

What should I bring to my first session?

Wear comfortable athletic clothes and proper training shoes. Bring a water bottle and a towel. If you have any medical conditions or take medications that affect exercise, please bring that information to discuss.

Do I need to be in shape before starting?

Not at all! You're hiring me to help you GET in shape. I'll meet you wherever you are and build a program that's appropriate for your current fitness level. That's the whole point of personal training.

What if I have injuries or physical limitations?

We'll work around them! Let me know about any injuries, surgeries, or conditions during our initial assessment. I'll modify exercises as needed and work with your healthcare providers if necessary to ensure safe, effective training.

Results & Expectations

How long before I see results?

You'll feel different within 2-3 weeks - more energy, better mood, improved sleep. You'll start noticing physical changes (strength gains, better endurance) within 4-6 weeks. Visible body composition changes typically show up around 8-12 weeks with consistent training and nutrition.

Everyone's timeline is different based on starting point, consistency, nutrition, and lifestyle factors. The key is staying consistent and trusting the process.

How often should I train per week?

For most people, 3-4 sessions per week is the sweet spot for great results without burnout. If you're just starting out, 2-3 times per week is perfect. More advanced clients might train 4-5 times weekly.

Consistency matters more than frequency - training twice a week every week beats training 5 times one week and zero the next.

Do you provide nutrition guidance?

Yes! Nutrition is included in the Transformation and Elite packages. I'll provide general nutrition guidance, meal planning ideas, and accountability. I focus on sustainable eating habits that support your training and fit your lifestyle.

I'm not a registered dietitian, so I don't provide medical nutrition therapy. For specific medical conditions or complex dietary needs, I'll refer you to a qualified professional.

What if I'm not seeing the results I want?

We'll troubleshoot together. Usually it comes down to consistency, nutrition, sleep, or stress management. I'll help identify what's holding you back and adjust your program accordingly. Progress isn't always linear, but if we're not moving in the right direction, we'll change the approach.
