Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in the free consultation?

During your free 30-minute consultation, we'll discuss your fitness goals, current activity level, any health considerations, and I'll explain how my training programs can help you achieve your goals. There's no pressure to sign up - it's simply a chance for us to see if we're a good fit.

How long are the training sessions?

Personal training sessions are 60 minutes. This includes a proper warm-up, the main workout, and a cool-down/stretch. Group training sessions are 45 minutes.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?

No contracts! All packages are month-to-month. You can cancel anytime with 7 days notice. I believe in earning your business every month through great results.

What if I need to reschedule a session?

Life happens! You can reschedule sessions with 24 hours notice at no charge. Sessions cancelled with less than 24 hours notice will be counted as completed.

Do you offer a money-back guarantee?

If you're not satisfied with your first session, I'll give you a full refund. I'm that confident you'll love training with me.
